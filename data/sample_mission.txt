
Mission Name:
Europa Exploration Mission


Target:
Europa - Jupiter's Moon


Mission Objective:
Explore Europa's surface and collect scientific data
to identify possible signs of water resources
and improve understanding of its environment.


Mission Constraints:

- Limited communication speed due to distance from Earth
- High radiation environment around Jupiter
- Limited spacecraft energy supply
- Long mission duration


Available Resources:

- Orbital spacecraft
- Scientific sensors
- Imaging equipment
- Communication system


Current Mission Status:

The spacecraft has successfully entered Europa's orbit.
The engineering team needs assistance with mission
planning, risk analysis, and resource optimization.
